Dynamic Account Maintenance: Easing the cost of compliance

Trintech

What began as a problem many viewed as impractical, seen as nearly impossible to lift because of requirement complexity and constraints already in the app, became a configurable product capability that automatically evaluated account risk and adjusted its reconciliation frequency through business-defined criteria.

It established a new risk-based approach to balance-sheet certification, gave customers a business justification for moving beyond inefficient (static) reconciliation policies, and became a key differentiator changing how the product was positioned in competitive sales cycles.

Verbatims

  • “The use of DAM has really reduced our reconciliations by 46%, which equates to about 1,100 recons on an annual basis. It saves our team anywhere from about 750 to 1,000 hours.” Carrie Goose, Texas Roadhouse
  • “People are spending the time on high-risk accounts, where they can add the most value, instead of just looking at the same accounts over and over and treating them as they’re all the same.” Cheryl Levesque, Forvis Mazars

Outcome

  • First-to-market risk-based reconciliation capability
  • Converted a highly customized service capability into a repeatable product feature
  • Sales created their first Partner Program, bringing a route to market the company did not have to staff

What it shows: Strategic Product Thinking • Product Strategy • Systems Design • Complex Systems Thinking • Business-to-Technical Translation

AI-powered English coaching app

Project

App transcribes speech, then provides grammar error corrections and leveled vocabulary/grammar in real time. GECToR (RoBERTa encoder via PyTorch/transformers) runs inference for proposing grammar error correction; spaCy provides POS and lemma features; ERRANT annotates edits with rules. Developed with Grok Build TUI under my direction: discovery, architecture choices, and site reviews.
